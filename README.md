## 🐾About Pawsitive Pet Hospital**

It is a full-stack veterinary clinic website and pet e-commerce platform, developed by **Nexora Solvex** as our **second client project**. It brings together clinic information, online shopping, customer accounts and a separate management dashboard — supported by an **AI website chatbot**, a **context-aware WhatsApp admin agent**, automated emails, PDF receipts and scheduled payment follow-ups.

Built around a practical clinic workflow, the system helps the team confirm availability, verify payments and keep customers informed through one connected platform.

## ✨ Features

--------------------

### 🏥 Clinic Website & Customer Guidance

--------------------------------------

- Responsive clinic website with a consistent orange visual theme.
- Dedicated information for **treatment, surgeries, boarding, grooming, laboratory testing and X-ray services**.
- Clinic contact details, location map and direct WhatsApp access.
- A **Guide** section explaining shopping, order tracking, payment verification and contacting the clinic.
- Branded navigation, clinic imagery and testimonials.

### 🛒 Pet Shopping Experience

--------------------------------------

- Browse **cat food, dog food, pet accessories and other pet essentials**.
- Product detail pages with images, descriptions, prices and supported variants.
- Customer **Cart** and **Wishlist**, connected to account data.
- Multi-item checkout with customer contact and delivery details.
- Prices displayed in **Pakistani Rupees — PKR / Rs**.

### 🔐 Accounts & Verified Order Access

-----------------------------------------------

- Customer registration, login and account management.
- Separate protected admin login and signed session cookies.
- Customer order history and status tracking through **My Orders**.
- **Six-digit email OTP verification** for chatbot access to eligible checkout orders.
- Server-generated OTPs with hashed verification, expiry and attempt limits; delivered through **Brevo**.

### 📦 Orders & Manual Payment Verification

-----------------------------

- A simple customer-facing workflow: **Checking Availability → Pending Payment → Paid**.
- Admin availability confirmation before asking the customer to pay.
- Configurable payment instructions and guidance for sending payment evidence to the clinic.
- Admin-confirmed payment updates, with automatic customer notifications.
- Unavailable orders can be cancelled while preserving their dashboard history.
- Dashboard controls provide a manual route when WhatsApp is unavailable.

> **Payment verification remains an admin responsibility.** The system updates the record after confirmation; it does not independently verify bank or wallet transactions. The MVP does not include courier tracking or proof of parcel delivery.

### 🖥️ Private Admin Dashboard

---------------------------

**Authorized clinic access only. Dashboard links are not publicly shared.**

--------------------------

- Manage products, categories, variants, images and pricing.
- Review customer orders and access receipts.
- A single action button follows the order state: **Check Availability → Mark as Paid → hidden after payment**.
- Automatic order refresh and sales/customer metrics.
- Email and WhatsApp submission indicators for operational visibility.
- Shared guarded order actions for dashboard controls and the WhatsApp agent.

### 🤖 AI Website Assistant

---------------------------------

- Answers clinic-service questions from the supplied knowledge content.
- Reads available product information and pricing from the application database.
- Supports English and Roman Urdu conversation.
- Lists product names concisely when requested and answers follow-up questions.
- Provides verified order information after the required access checks.
- Uses clinic contact guidance when information is unconfirmed or veterinary attention is needed.
- Integrates **Jina AI reranking** to improve selection of relevant knowledge content when configured.

### 💬 Context-Aware WhatsApp Admin Agent

------------------------------------

- Sends new-order information and asks the admin to confirm availability.
- Asks whether payment has arrived and applies authorized order updates.
- Uses the active question, recent conversation and order references to interpret natural-language replies.
- Supports availability/payment confirmations, order queries and sales summaries.
- Treats acknowledgements such as **“good”** and **“thanks”** as conversation rather than payment approval.
- Retrieves recorded order facts from the database instead of generating payment totals or statuses from memory.
- Includes provider timeout handling, response validation, bounded retries and configured fallback support.

### 📧 Automated Emails, WhatsApp Updates & Receipts

----------------------------------

- Customer emails for order acknowledgement, availability confirmation and payment confirmation.
- WhatsApp order updates and generated **PDF receipts**.
- Separate delivery markers for email, WhatsApp text and PDF submissions.
- Retry handling for failed channels without routinely resending channels already recorded as successful.
- Email processing remains independent of admin WhatsApp availability.

> A successful provider submission is not proof that a customer read the message. Delivery depends on the configured providers, account limits and connectivity.

### ⏰ Scheduled Follow-Ups & Background Processing

--------------------------------------

- Scheduled admin availability reminders and payment checks.
- Configurable reminder timing and retry intervals.
- Durable webhook jobs and a database-backed worker lease to coordinate background work.
- An authenticated cron endpoint used by an external scheduler.
- Follow-up processing continues without requiring the dashboard browser tab to stay open, while the deployed worker and scheduler are operating.

### 🔎 SEO & Discoverability

-----------------------------

- Relevant clinic, veterinary-service and pet-product keyword integration.
- Page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure and internal linking.
- Canonical URL configuration and social-sharing metadata.
- **`robots.txt`** and **`sitemap.xml`** for crawler guidance and URL discovery.
- **Google Search Console** verification and sitemap submission.
- Clear, answer-oriented service and guidance content supporting **AEO foundations**.

> Search rankings, indexing and inclusion in AI-generated answers are determined by the respective platforms; SEO/AEO setup does not guarantee placement.
